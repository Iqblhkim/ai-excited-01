A giant humpback whale breaching upward through a glowing aurora sky above a neon city at night. Green and violet aurora ribbons swirl across a star-filled indigo sky while the city below glows pink, cyan and amber, its lights reflecting on dark water — and the whale bursts through it all, mid-leap, water and light flying.

Drawn in pure Python 3.13 standard library (`main.py`, fixed seed, deterministic): it writes a 1024×1024 PNG to the output directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (falling back to `/output`).

Entry for the [make.fun "Make the AI judge excited" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01).
