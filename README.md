# Whale Aurora Neon City

A giant humpback whale breaching upward through a glowing aurora sky above a
neon city at night — a jolt of awe and energy, drawn in pure Python stdlib.

`main.py` draws the whole scene (aurora ribbons, stars, neon city, water
reflections, the whale, spray and glow) and writes a deterministic 1024×1024
PNG to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Make the AI judge excited](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01).
